---
title: "Christians Are Called to Enter the Communion “Prepared from the Foundation of the World”"
description: "The Church as a communion of sanctification and salvation, existing in Christ before all ages."
translationKey: "epikaira-etoimasmeni-koinonia"
date: 2011-01-07
weight: 50
---

The Orthodox faithful, as members of the Church, do not strive to create a communion, but are called to enter the communion “prepared from the foundation of the world”.[^n1] The Church, as a communion of sanctification and salvation, exists before all ages in Christ. Its beginning and origin lie in God. For this reason the communion of the Church “is not of this world”.[^n2] It is “the mystery hidden from the ages in God”.[^n3] It pre-existed creation in the eternal will of God, just as God’s plan for our salvation is before all ages. According to Athanasius the Great, the Church, “having been created first, is afterwards begotten of God”.

[^n1]: Matt 25:34.
[^n2]: John 18:36.
[^n3]: Eph 3:9.
