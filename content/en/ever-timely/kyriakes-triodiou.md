All the Sundays of the Triodion have a specific name and form a sequence rich in meaning and message. In order, the Sundays of the Triodion are:

1. Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee
2. Sunday of the Prodigal Son
3. Meatfare Sunday (of the Last Judgement)
4. Cheesefare Sunday (Forgiveness Sunday)
5. First Sunday of Lent: Sunday of Orthodoxy
6. Second Sunday of Lent: Saint Gregory Palamas
7. Third Sunday of Lent: Veneration of the Holy Cross
8. Fourth Sunday of Lent: Saint John of the Ladder (Climacus)
9. Fifth Sunday of Lent: Saint Mary of Egypt
10. Palm Sunday

See also [The Triodion](/en/ever-timely/triodio/).
