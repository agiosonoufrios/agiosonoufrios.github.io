---
title: "“Why Are You Sad?”"
description: "How can we be sad, when Christ is risen?"
translationKey: "epikaira-ti-este-skythropoi"
date: 2012-01-08
weight: 150
---

“Why are you sad?”[^s1] How can we still be gloomy, sorrowful, melancholy? Christ our Lord is risen! If all creation leaps with joy at the Resurrection of Christ, how can the winter of this world still weigh down our hearts and our faces? If the risen Christ walks and the earth brings forth roses, how is it possible that He walks beside us and thorns still pierce our souls? After His resurrection our Lord assured us that He would be with us all the days of our life, until the end of the world.[^s2]

[^s1]: Luke 24:17.
[^s2]: Cf. Matt 28:20.
