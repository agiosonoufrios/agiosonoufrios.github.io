---
title: "The Mystery of the Priesthood"
description: "The Mystery of the Priesthood: a God-given office, the divine calling and apostolic succession."

The Mystery of the Priesthood is a divine office, because it is not chiefly men but God, through men – the bishops – who calls and consecrates His ministers in His Church. When Paul summoned the presbyters of the Church of Ephesus to Miletus to urge them to complete vigilance over the life and conduct of their Christians, he said to them: “Take heed to yourselves and to all the flock, among which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to shepherd the Church of the Lord and God.”[^n1] It is, then, a God-given, divine office and not a worldly one. “The priesthood is performed on earth,” as Saint John Chrysostom says, “but it ranks among heavenly things.” For this reason one must not hasten to this office, the priesthood, but wait – either through an inner inclination, which is not always safe, or through chosen persons and favourable circumstances – for God to call him to this highest ministry. It is true that the divine saying of Paul is inspired and worthy of attention: “If anyone desires the office of a bishop, he desires a good work.”[^n2] But another inspired word of Paul is also this: “No one takes this honour (of the priesthood) upon himself, but he who is called by God, just as Aaron was.”[^n3] Let no one hasten, then, and much less use means and bribes, by which he becomes guilty of “simony” and therefore unworthy of the honour and grace. Those who take on the responsibility of ordaining a candidate “must first examine the life of the one being ordained, and only then invoke upon him the grace of the Holy Spirit”. So did the holy Apostles when they were to choose the seven deacons. They called the multitude of the faithful and said to them: “Seek out from among you seven men of good reputation.”[^n4] Men of good testimony, that is, faithful, modest, holy, worthy of this grace and ministry. For only the holy and the worthy should be chosen and advanced to the degrees of the priesthood.

The Priesthood is a Mystery to which one comes by divine calling. The Mystery of the Priesthood was also instituted by God; that is, the Lord Himself instituted it. He instituted it when He set apart His Twelve Apostles to be His closest circle and afterwards His successors and the continuers of His work. Above all, however, when after His Resurrection and on the day of Pentecost He imparted to them His Holy Spirit and handed over to them the authority to forgive sins, as we saw in the Mystery of repentance, which was precisely the authority of the priesthood. His holy Apostles afterwards did the same for their own succession: “When they had ordained elders for them in every church, and prayed with fasting, they commended them to the Lord in whom they had believed.”[^n5]
