---
title: "Breaking the Bonds of Exhausting Work and Wealth"

Breaking the bonds that tie man to exhausting work and to wealth is a difficult matter in our anxious, consumer-driven age, but it can become a reality through the grace of Christ.

<p class="signature">This page contains a small tribute to Saint Onuphrius, my protector, who through his entreaties to the Lord has helped me, and helps me, in difficult circumstances.<br><span>Jason Digalakis</span></p>
