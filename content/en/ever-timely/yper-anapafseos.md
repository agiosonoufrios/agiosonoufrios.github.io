The Fathers of the Church have appointed the Saturday before Meatfare Sunday and the Saturday before Pentecost as Saturdays of Souls, days dedicated to the souls of our departed brethren. On these days above all, then, the Church celebrates the holy Memorial Services for her children who rest in Christ.

Through the memorial services the Church calls on the faithful to show their love for their departed brother, so that his sins may be forgiven; to pray for him together with the Church to the Lord of the living and the dead; and to ask for him the abundance of divine grace and the inheritance of the heavenly kingdom.

When we wish to have a memorial service, we should bring to church the prosphoron, the wine and the names of the departed, so that they may first be commemorated at the Divine Liturgy. And we take care to take part in the Divine Liturgy ourselves and to be ready to approach the Mystery of the Holy Eucharist, for at that moment we unite ourselves with Christ and also with our departed brethren. At that sacred moment, when we have Christ before us in the Holy Chalice, we can pray and fervently ask for the forgiveness of our brethren. At the end of the Divine Liturgy the special memorial prayer will be read over the <em>kollyva</em> we have offered. The boiled and broken grains of wheat represent the departed. Just as buried seeds prepare to sprout in spring, so the souls of the departed await the day of the common Resurrection in order to rise. We should also accompany our prayers with almsgiving for the departed.
