---
title: "The Triodion"
description: "The period of the Triodion: its characteristics, name and meaning."
translationKey: "epikaira-triodio"
date: 2011-02-20

---

An ecclesiastical period extending from the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee up to and including Holy Saturday. Its particular characteristic, from which it also takes its name, is that during this period the Canons of the daily services consist of three odes and not nine, as is the case with all other Canons. It is full of services, offering the faithful many opportunities for liturgical experience. It is a period of compunction and of preparation for Holy Pascha and the Resurrection of the Lord. It does not always fall at the same time of year, since it depends on the date of Pascha.
