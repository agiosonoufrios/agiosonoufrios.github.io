<p>It should be kneaded at a time when, if possible, no one else is in the house. We do not listen to the radio or watch television, and we have confessed the sins that weigh upon us. We cense our space, in this case the kitchen; our oil lamp is lit; we also light the candle before the icon of the Theotokos, and we begin. It should be stressed that we must use sourdough leaven and not yeast. Also, for the kneading we keep special towels and special utensils, which we do not use for other purposes, for example for sweets or food.</p>

<p>We make a cross in the flour and place the leaven in it. We say the “Our Father” and begin kneading, during which we say either the Salutations to the Theotokos or the “Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos”. Our dough must become quite firm, not like bread dough, because inside the prosphoron must be compact, without air bubbles, and not over-baked. Before placing it in the baking tin, which is greased not with oil but with pure wax, we make a small cross of dough, place it in the centre of the tin, then place the dough on top of it, and we finish by pressing the seal. The seal must be wooden and deeply carved, so that its imprint is clear and clean. We must take care that the holes we make to let out any air during baking are not made within the outline formed by the cross with the portions of the Lord, the Theotokos and the Saints.</p>

<p>When it is baked and we take it out of the oven, we let it cool a little, wrap it in a clean towel and place it in a plastic bag so that it becomes soft and not dry. To carry it to church we use a clean towel, if possible with a cross embroidered on it, so that it is used only for our prosphoron.</p>

Ἡ σφραγίδα πρέπει νά εἶναι ξύλινη καί μέ βαθιά χαράγματα ὥστε τό ἐκτύπωμά της νά εἶναι εὐκρινές καί καθαρό. Θά πρέπει νά προσέξουμε ὥστε οἱ τρύπες, τίς ὁποῖες κάνουμε γιά νά βγεῖ ὁ τυχόν ἀέρας κατά τό ψήσιμο, νά μήν γίνονται μέσα στό περίγραμμα πού δημιουργεῖ ὁ σταυρός μέ τίς μερίδες τοῦ Κυρίου, τῆς Παναγίας καί τῶν Ἁγίων.</p><p lang="el">Ἀφοῦ ψηθεῖ καί τό βγάλουμε ἀπό τό φοῦρνο, τό ἀφήνουμε λίγο νά κρυώσει καί τό τυλίγουμε μέ μιά πετσέτα καθαρή καί τό βάζουμε μέσα σέ πλαστική σακκούλα γιά νά γίνει μαλακό καί ὄχι ξερό.
