The Holy Proskomide is the first part of the Divine Liturgy, in which the bread and wine are prepared at the Holy Prothesis and the living and the departed are commemorated. The following pages bring together short texts on the preparation of the prosphoron (the offering bread) and its symbolism, together with the relevant encyclical of the Church of Greece. See also <a href="/en/synaxarion/divine-liturgy/">The Divine Liturgy</a> and <a href="/en/synaxarion/divine-liturgy/diataxi/">The Order of the Divine Liturgy</a>.
