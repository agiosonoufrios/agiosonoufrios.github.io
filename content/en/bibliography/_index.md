This page brings together all the sources and reference works used in the texts of this website: liturgical and hagiographical books, Holy Scripture and patristic texts, studies, articles and online sources. The entries follow the <em>Chicago Manual of Style</em> (17th ed.) and are arranged alphabetically within each category. Works are cited in their original Greek.

<p class="note"><strong>Use in research.</strong> The “Download bibliography” button downloads all entries as a BibTeX file (<code>vivliografia.bib</code>, UTF-8 encoding). The file can be imported directly into Zotero (<em>File → Import</em>), JabRef and Mendeley, and used in LaTeX with <code>biblatex</code> and <code>biber</code>.</p>
