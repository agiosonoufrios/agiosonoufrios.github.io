This website is dedicated to Saint Onuphrius the Egyptian, one of the great ascetic figures of the Egyptian desert. It presents the life and hymnography of the Saint, selected patristic and liturgical texts, and the sources and bibliography on which they are based.

<p class="note">The website is being transferred gradually to its new form. The English texts are in preparation; the previous (Greek) version remains available at <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/agiosonouphrios/">sites.google.com/site/agiosonouphrios</a>.</p>
